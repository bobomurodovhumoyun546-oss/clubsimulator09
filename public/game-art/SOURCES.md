# Football card backgrounds

Downloaded photographs from Unsplash's image CDN. These are decorative card
backgrounds, not questions or clues. The same photographs are framed at different
positions on different game cards. Originals are kept locally as JPEGs;
framing, dark gradients and hover effects are applied in CSS.
